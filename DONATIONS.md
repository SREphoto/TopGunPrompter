# Support the TopGun Prompter Project

**TopGun Prompter** is a labor of love built for the AI art community. Curating thousands of movie scenes and engineering specific visual styles leads to hundreds of hours of work. If this tool has helped you create stunning art, consider supporting the project!

![Support the Project](support_the_project.png)

### Why Support Us?

Your contributions go directly towards:

- **Expanding the Library**: Helping us reach the "50-Scene Standard" for every single movie in our database.
- **Server Costs**: keeping the web version fast and accessible for everyone.
- **Feature Development**: Implementing new AI model support (flux, stable diffusion xl, etc.) and advanced prompt weighting.
