TopGun Prompter / Cinema Archive

ABOUT
-----
Cinema Archive (formerly TopGun Prompter) is a premium prompt generation 
tool for AI image creators. It provides curated scene descriptions and 
visual styles inspired by 300+ films, TV series, and video games.

KEY FEATURES
------------
• 300+ Movies, TV Shows, and Video Games
• 50 High-Detail Scenes per Elite Title
• Unique Visual Styles per Film
• Midjourney V7 Optimized Prompts
• Auto-Copy & Batch Generation Modes
• Christmas, War, and Themed Visual Effects
